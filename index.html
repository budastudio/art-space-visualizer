About the space

Art Space Visualizer is an evolving companion to the Buda Studio Store — a place to see the work before you decide how to live with it. This first stage is a study in composition: header, light, wall, and label. What the space becomes next — depth, motion, a room you can walk through — will be built on what holds up here first.
